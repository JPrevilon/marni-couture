# $1,000 Initial Marketing Plan

## Objective

Use the existing TikTok account as the primary discovery channel, establish credible Instagram and Snapchat presences, drive qualified traffic to the new website, and identify the first creative format capable of generating product views, add-to-carts, and purchases.

## Budget

| Use | Budget |
|---|---:|
| TikTok Spark Ads and website conversion tests | $600 |
| Instagram/Meta Reels and retargeting | $250 |
| Creative testing and contingency | $150 |
| Snapchat paid media | $0 initially |
| **Total** | **$1,000** |

Snapchat launches organically first. Dividing a small budget across three paid platforms before the new accounts have content would weaken the test.

## 30-day sequence

### Days 1–7 — Foundation

- Publish or refresh the TikTok bio and website link.
- Create the client-owned Instagram and Snapchat accounts.
- Prepare at least nine Instagram launch posts.
- Publish daily or near-daily short content.
- Confirm website and pixel events in test mode.
- Do not spend paid media until checkout and product data are ready.

### Days 8–14 — Organic validation

Test three creative concepts:

1. Mannequin to motion
2. Product detail, fit, and movement
3. Making, styling, or packing

Create at least two opening hooks for each concept. Select winners based on watch quality, profile visits, website clicks, product views, and comment intent rather than views alone.

### Days 15–23 — Paid test

- Promote one or two proven TikTok posts through Spark Ads.
- Start around $20–$30 per day.
- Launch a smaller Instagram Reels test.
- Retarget site visitors only after a useful audience exists.
- Stop any ad that generates weak traffic or no product engagement.

### Days 24–30 — Concentration

- Move remaining spend toward the strongest post and product.
- Publish reply videos answering real customer questions.
- Show packing, availability, fit, and shipping facts.
- Build the next month around the best hook and strongest product.

## Content pillars

### Mannequin to Motion

Transform still mannequin imagery into a moving editorial reveal.

### Made by Marni

Show sketches, fabric selection, sewing, embellishment, fitting, styling, and packing.

### Style the Drop

Show one item styled several ways or respond to audience styling prompts.

### Drop Signals

Use truthful launch, preorder, low-stock, restock, and countdown information.

### Community in Couture

Publish approved customer styling, reactions, reviews, creator content, and unboxings.

## Initial cadence

| Channel | Cadence |
|---|---|
| TikTok | 5 posts per week and optional live session |
| Instagram Reels | 3 per week |
| Instagram carousel/static | 2 per week |
| Instagram Stories | Most active days |
| Snapchat Stories | 4–7 active days per week |
| Snapchat Spotlight | 1–2 polished vertical clips per week |
| Email | 1 regular message per week plus drop messages |

## Measurement

Track:

- Social profile visits
- Website sessions by platform
- Product views
- Add-to-cart rate
- Checkout-start rate
- Purchase conversion
- Average order value
- Cost per add to cart
- Customer acquisition cost
- Revenue by campaign
- Email signup rate
- Returning visitor rate

Use UTMs consistently.

```text
utm_source=tiktok
utm_medium=organic
utm_campaign=first_motion
utm_content=mannequin_reveal_01
```

Do not scale spending based only on views or follower growth.
