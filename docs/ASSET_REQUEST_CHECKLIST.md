# Client Asset and Information Request

## Brand

- Confirm exact public spelling
- Confirm legal business name
- Domain preference
- Final logo approval
- Designer biography
- Founder portrait
- Studio and process photography
- Public customer-service email
- Business address where required

## Per product

- Product name
- SKU
- Price
- Compare-at price if used
- Sizes
- Colors
- Inventory
- Product weight
- Materials
- Care
- Fit notes
- Garment measurements
- Ready-to-ship, preorder, or made-to-order status
- Fulfillment time
- Return eligibility
- Three to six original photographs
- One vertical movement video
- One detail or process clip

## Commerce

- Shopify store owner access
- Payment setup
- Tax region
- Shipping regions
- Shipping rates
- Carriers
- Return window
- Exchange rules
- Final-sale rules
- Refund timing
- Discount strategy

## Social and marketing

- Instagram handle
- Snapchat handle
- Meta Business access
- TikTok Business access
- Snapchat Business access
- Approved ad account owner
- Creator or customer permissions
- Email platform choice
- Analytics owner account
